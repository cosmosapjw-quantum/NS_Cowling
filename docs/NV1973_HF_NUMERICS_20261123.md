# NV1973 orbital Hartree–Fock numerical implementation

## Scope and source identity

`compose_eos_nv_hf.f90` solves a spherical, zero-temperature, unpaired orbital
Hartree–Fock cell for specified neutron number N, proton number Z and mean baryon
density. It calls the NV1972 DME functional with the NV1973 bulk potential.
Thomas–Fermi tau is used only to initialize the SCF iteration; final densities
and kinetic densities come from actual occupied radial eigenvectors.

Sources: Negele and Vautherin, Phys. Rev. C **5**, 1472 (1972),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevC.5.1472; Nucl. Phys. A **207**, 298 (1973),
DOI 10.1016/0375-9474(73)90349-7. Source coefficients and remaining ambiguity are
documented in `NV1972_DME_FUNCTIONAL_20261123.md`.

Mode 0 reproduces the printed coefficient table. Mode 1 is the conditional
`NV1973_DME_D03x100_diagnostic`, changing only D03 and D30 from −6.62699×10^3 to
−6.62699×10^5 MeV fm^14. It is not an author-confirmed erratum or an exact
reproduction of the authors' calculation. Mode calls share no mutable state.

## Radial discretization

For radius R and m radial cells of width h, cell centers are r_i=(i−1/2)h and
radial volume weights are w_i=[(ih)^3−((i−1)h)^3]/3. Radial R_l=u_l/r obeys
sum_i w_i R_l(r_i)^2=1. The eigensolver uses x_i=sqrt(w_i)R_l(r_i).

With a_q=hbar²/(2m)+B_q, the symmetric finite-volume face stiffness is
g_(i+1/2)=(ih)^2[a_i+a_(i+1)]/(2h). Diagonals receive (g_left+g_right)/w_i,
off-diagonals receive −g_face/sqrt(w_i w_(i+1)), and the angular term is
a_i l(l+1)h/w_i. This is the exact variation of the stated discrete kinetic
energy. It includes the a'(r)/r term present in the reduced-u equation.

Even-l radial R vanishes at the cell boundary, adding stiffness 2R²a/h. Odd-l
R' vanishes, giving zero boundary flux, equivalent to u'(R)=u(R)/R. At the
origin the radial flux weight vanishes. This is the mixed neutron boundary
condition on NV1973 pp. 309–310. The same family is applied to bound proton
states; boundary tails need scrutiny if a state is interpreted as a cluster.

Each face contribution to orbital kinetic density is split equally between its
neighboring volumes, making its effective-mass variation the eigenproblem above.
Neutron degeneracy is 2(2l+1), without neutron spin–orbit splitting. Protons use
j=l±1/2 with degeneracy 2j+1. A partially occupied final multiplet uses spherical
equal filling. The proton spin density is computed from these eigenvectors.

The centered density-gradient operator D uses reflected endpoint values. Its
contribution to the central potential is W^−1 D^T W q, where q=∂H/∂(D rho).
This is a discrete adjoint, not an independently chosen divergence stencil.
The functional includes H_so=(W0/2) grad(rho_n+2rho_p)·J_p. Its neutron central
potential contribution is retained even though neutron orbital spin–orbit
splitting is omitted.

The Coulomb direct functional uses the symmetric spherical-shell kernel
1/max(r_i,r_j) on charge rho_p−Z/V. Its energy is half the charge–potential
contraction. Proton Slater exchange is included. Pairing, electron exchange,
screening corrections, non-spherical pasta and lattice bands are outside this
specified approximation.

LAPACK DSTEVX computes selected lowest eigenpairs. DSTEVR was replaced because
its ILAENV capability probe deliberately generates IEEE exceptional values;
DSTEVX permits invalid/zero/overflow traps to remain active in numerical checks.

## Thermodynamic quantities

Units are fm^−3 for rho, fm^−5 for tau, fm^−4 for J, MeV for energies and
MeV fm^−3 for pressure. N in NV1973 Table 3 counts neutrons, so A=N+Z. Energy
relative to free neutron rest energy is

E_offset = E_nuclear + E_Coulomb + E_e,total + Z (m_p−m_n)c².

Electron rest energy is counted once. Its Coulomb chemical-potential shift is
minus the volume average of the nuclear Coulomb potential.

Pressure is the exact discrete coordinate-dilation derivative at fixed orbital
occupations. Under r→s r: rho→rho/s³, tau→tau/s⁵, grad rho→grad rho/s⁴,
J→J/s⁴. Therefore

P = {integral [3 rho·U_local + 5 tau·a + 4 grad rho·q + 4 J_p W_p − 3H] dV
     + E_Coulomb,direct + E_Coulomb,exchange}/(3V) + P_e.

An independent symmetric energy dilation checks this expression. This is a
specified-cell pressure, not automatically the pressure of a globally minimized
beta-equilibrium EOS.

Neutron/proton occupied and addition frontier eigenvalues exclude rest masses.
At an integer shell closure, the first-order beta interval is

lower = mu_p,occupied − mu_n,addition + mu_e,total + (m_p−m_n)c²,
upper = mu_p,addition − mu_n,occupied + mu_e,total + (m_p−m_n)c².

The interval must contain zero for the addition/removal diagnostic. A nonzero
occupied-level beta residual alone does not disprove beta stability. Fully
relaxed neighboring-cell energies are a stronger composition check.

## Convergence and admission

SCF mixes rho, tau and J, preserves particle counts, and supports clustered,
uniform and diffuse seeds plus scaled same-grid warm continuation. Initial
cluster amplitude is bounded to respect the source-fit density domain.
Subsequent out-of-domain proposals are damped without changing the domain; a
vanishing admissible step is `HF_SCF_DOMAIN_STEP_TOO_SMALL`.

Convergence requires rho, tau and J fixed-point residuals below the configured
density tolerance, an energy-change tolerance, and no occupied state at the
selected angular or radial basis edge. Defaults are 2×10^−6 for normalized
fixed-point residuals and 2×10^−7 MeV per baryon for the energy change. A final
solve under recomputed raw orbital fields checks the fixed point again.
Mesh refinement, enlarged basis, seed comparisons, composition minimization
and source authenticity remain separate requirements.

Optional outer smoothing explicitly records an averaging-region convention and
particle renormalization while retaining raw orbitals. Such an iteration is not
silently admitted as a stationary point of the raw variational functional.
Default calculations use unsmoothed fields.

`energy_valid` distinguishes an evaluated energy from an early failure.
`converged` and `status=ok` must both hold for successful process exit. An
evaluated energy on an unconverged run is not a qualified EOS input.

## Reproducible native checks

Run `bash fortran_eos/tests/nv_hf_build.sh /tmp/nv_hf_tests`. The native suite has
43 free-spectrum, normalization, radial-refinement, gradient-adjoint and electron
checks, plus 16 independent orbital directional derivatives and 8 pressure
dilation comparisons in both coefficient modes: 67 checks in total.

Spherical Bessel zeros for even l and derivative zeros for odd l supply the
independent free-spectrum reference. Refining mesh 240→480 reduces its maximum
relative eigenvalue error from 1.2709723502×10^−4 to 3.1774745818×10^−5,
ratio 0.25000343881. Tested scaled orbital variational errors are below 4.0×10^−9.
GNU Fortran uses `-fcheck=all -ffpe-trap=invalid,zero,overflow`.

An additional native HF check increases (lmax, radial states) from (16,11) to
(18,13) for the mode-1 N=130, Z=50, mesh-160 cell. Warm continuation changes
energy by 4.24×10^−12 MeV per baryon and pressure by 2.08×10^−9 MeV fm^−3;
the final fixed-point residual is 2.59×10^−7. This closes the omitted-state
comparison at that specified cell, not at every possible cell.

The standalone native executable takes source path, output path, then N, Z, n_b,
mesh, maximum iterations, mixing, seed kind, outer-smoothing logical and integer
coefficient mode. For example:

```sh
build/compose_eos_fortran/nv_hf_probe \
  data/eos_original_papers_20261123/research_nv/nv1973_original_source.dat \
  /tmp/cell.txt 140 40 2.79e-4 320 1200 .08 0 F 1
```

Parallel campaigns use independent cell processes and one OMP/BLAS thread per
process. Python handles orchestration and receipts only.

## Preliminary observations

For the printed table, the tested first-cell clustered seed reaches the source
density boundary, whereas a uniform seed converges to a higher local stationary
state. The cluster failure therefore does not establish absence of HF solutions.

For the separately named D03x100 diagnostic, N=140, Z=40 converges at radial
meshes 160, 320 and 480, with E/A−m_n approximately −1.505971, −1.457575 and
−1.448753 MeV. This is material second-order mesh dependence. Clustered and
diffuse seeds agree in energy at mesh 320. The first-cell beta interval misses
zero, so energy proximity to the original table is not accepted as a source
identity or composition certificate.

In the 28-trial mode-1 campaign, all 11 published-composition clustered seeds
converge. Five of the 11 uniform seeds fail the unchanged iteration/residual
criteria, and all six A=180 charge trials Z=40,42,44,46,48,50 converge, giving
23 SCF passes total. The finite charge scan lowers energy as Z increases to 50;
only the Z=50 interval contains zero. The uniform and clustered branches can
be different local stationary states. Full phase-space minimization is not
established by these finite trials.

Three additional fully relaxed neighbors use the final main executable at
A=180, mesh 320, the same density, mixing 0.08 and maximum 1200 iterations.
Relative to Z=50, their cell energies increase by 0.335563871 MeV at Z=49,
5.072669765 MeV at Z=51 and 10.462765604 MeV at Z=52. Thus the Z=50 result is
a strict local minimum in this tested integer charge direction. It does not
minimize the cell's total baryon number A or resolve coefficient authenticity.

The preliminary printed-table campaign used mixing 0.03 and maximum 600
iterations; the main diagnostic and charge campaigns used mixing 0.08 and
maximum 1200. These settings are retained per campaign in the receipts and CSVs.
A final-front-end check at mixing 0.03 reached spin residual 2.2014×10^−6 at
iteration 600 and was correctly rejected despite an already stable energy.
This iteration-budget failure is preserved and is not relabeled a source or
implementation failure.

The dated NV DME results package preserves complete campaigns, hashes, failed
trials and final selection decisions. Fixed-composition HF success alone does
not authorize promotion to a matched EOS.
