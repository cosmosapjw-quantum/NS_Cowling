GM(GM1)

Abstract

This EoS is the classical RMF parameterisation GM1 [GMPR_1991] for cold neutron star matter in beta-equilibrium containing nucleons and electrons. For the crust, the EoS by Douchin and Haensel [DHA_2001] has been added below a density of nB = 10^{−3} fm^{−3}. Proton fraction and compositional information is available for the core only.
