RG(SLY4)

Abstract

This table corresponds to the zero temperature and beta-equilibrium unified EoS by Gulminelli and Raduta [GR_2015]. The considered effective interaction is SLy4 [CBHMS_1998]. Cluster energy functionals are those of Ref. [DLNP_2009].

Particles = npemN
T min = 0.0
T max = 0.0
T pts = 1
nb min = 1.00e-07
nb max = 1.21e+00
nb pts = 197
Y min = 0.0
Y max = 0.0
Y pts = 1

E0 = 15.97 Mev
K = 230.0 Mev
