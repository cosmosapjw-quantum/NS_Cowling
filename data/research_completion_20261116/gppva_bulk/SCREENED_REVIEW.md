# Independent screened-electron review

The reviewed final `spatial_tf_equilibrium.py` SHA-256 is `559f62e1fec635e38d1a098d0d1d7831b6e57c83ed4672c0b18a141609c34a79`. The production physics files were read-only for this reviewer. Evidence below supports local discrete functional consistency and the specified numerical cases; it does not prove a global cell minimum or convergence outside sampled meshes.

For proton profile n_p and radial cell radius R, the electron subproblem minimizes electron kinetic plus Coulomb energy at <n_e>=<n_p>. With the electrostatic potential V in MeV, the occupied-electron condition is sqrt((hbar*c*k_F)^2+m_e^2)-V=mu_e,ref. The Poisson problem has natural zero-flux boundaries, its constant nullspace is removed by <V>=0, and global neutrality supplies compatibility. The independently assembled augmented Poisson/gauge linear solve reproduces the production pseudoinverse potential. Coulomb charge energy <(n_p-n_e)V>/2 agrees with its field-quadratic form.

The reduced density derivative is mu_n for neutrons and mu_p+mu_e,ref for protons. This follows because the full functional differential contains <mu_e(r) delta n_e>, while the eliminated solution has constant mu_e,ref and delta<n_e>=delta<n_p>. The radius derivative receives no implicit electron term at fixed proton densities because the mean charge constraint is radius-independent in the normalized radial grid. The full discrete chain rule and direct finite differences test these statements, including directions that change total proton number.

Across all eight models and all three geometries (24 functionals, four directions each), the final raw h-refined energy derivative error is 2.211e-6. The finite Poisson tolerance leaves tiny charge residuals whose derivative contributes at this scale. Evaluating the constrained Lagrangian E+mu_e,ref*<(n_p-n_e)> solely for diagnosis reduces the finite-difference error to 1.452e-7 and the full chain-rule mismatch to 1.959e-14. No charge-based energy correction is applied in production. All raw values and corrections are retained in `screened_functional_review.json`.
