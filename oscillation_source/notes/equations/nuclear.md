#  수식표 수식과 범위

- `u=n/n0`, `alpha=1-2x_p`.
- Symmetric kinetic contribution: degenerate neutron/proton Fermi seas.
- Phenomenological symmetric interaction is calibrated to `E(n0)=-B`, `P(n0)=0`, and `K0` after the functional form (and, for the regulated model, the regulator) is selected.
- Parabolic asymmetric matter: `e(n,alpha)=e0(n)+S(n) alpha^2`.
- npe beta equilibrium: `4 S(n) alpha = mu_e`, `n_e=n_p=x_p n`.
- Electron gas is zero-temperature, ideal, and relativistic.
- Total energy density contains the common nucleon rest-energy term plus nuclear excess energy and electron energy.

Not included: muons, RMF, hyperons, quarks, elastic crust, or a realistic core-crust transition.
