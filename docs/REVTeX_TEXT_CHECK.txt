 Quadrupolar f - and p1 -mode frequencies of joined neutron-star equations of state in
                        the relativistic Cowling approximation

                We calculate the quadrupolar polar-fluid fundamental mode and first pressure overtone of 234
             crust–core joined neutron-star equations of state (EOSs). The cohort is drawn from a 390-member
             matching matrix containing 13 crust tables, three high-density core families, and six matching pre-
             scriptions; the 156 cases independently classified as retaining source-core acausality are excluded.
             Before stellar integration, we construct a separate solver-adapter representation by taking q(
             muB ) = nB /
             muB piecewise linear, integrating P ′ = nB =
             muB q, and setting
             epsilon =
             muB nB − P . All 234 source q sequences are already nondecreasing, so weighted isotonic regres-
             sion pools zero knots; the nontrivial operation is the between-knot closure and pressure/energy
             re-integration. The original EOS tables are preserved separately. For each EOS we solve the
                                                                                    N
             TOV equations at a fixed gravitational mass M = 1.3300106263 M⊙          and locate the ℓ = 2 fluid
             roots by two-sided matching in the relativistic Cowling approximation. Eigenfunction node counts,
             rather than frequency ordering alone, identify the f and p1 modes. All 234 cases pass the node-
             identity and matching checks. Across the three core families, the median frequencies are respectively
             (f, p1 ) = (2.505, 7.008) kHz for BBB BHF2, (2.370, 6.718) kHz for RG SLY4, and (1.920, 6.009) kHz
             for GM GM1. Tightening the ODE tolerances and step cap changes the frequencies of three represen-
             tative models by at most 1.71 × 10−6 Hz between the loose and tight runs. These small integration
             differences quantify numerical convergence within the adopted model; they do not quantify the
             systematic error of the Cowling approximation or the uncertainty associated with the barotropic,
             beta-equilibrated perturbation closure.
