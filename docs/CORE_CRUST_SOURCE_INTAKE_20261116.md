## Source facts and candidate

- [Scurto et al. v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.15548v1), Eqs. 20–25 and 31: bulk uses sigma, omega and rho couplings, with no delta meson, `g_i=a_i+(b_i+d_i x^3) exp(-c_i x)` and `x=rho/rho_sat`. Each realization has 12 coupling parameters. Surface `sigma_0,b` are optimized per bulk model. Eq. 31 describes an average squared mass discrepancy with an uncertainty stated as 2% of the corresponding mass; the paper says 2,408 masses. The source's stated objective is useful but does not supply the executable fitting protocol, selected row identities, convention for atomic-to-nuclear mass conversion, or model-specific fitted output.
- The [CompOSE SPG M1 datasheet](https://compose.obspm.fr/download/1D/Crust/M1/eos.pdf) identifies a posterior-selected GDFM model and a BSk22 outer crust, not a numeric M1 coupling vector or surface fit. The paper's Figure 19 shows coupling curves. Registry `data/eos390_rmf_parameter_sets.v1.json` documents placeholders and surrogates. Similarity to a prior-center model does not establish parameter identity. For each desired SPG M1–M5 realization, acquire its exact 12 coefficients and rho_sat with parameter/meson conventions, and its fitted sigma_0 and b (or the complete source fit and input provenance). GPPVA models likewise require their own model-specific fit; none is inferred here.
- Local read-only CompOSE archive inspection: `317.zip` (M3, SHA-256 `dd70e8806d8da52b475791459478e1ff2cfb507e7c3815916dabbc7de4627885`) and `319.zip` (M5, SHA-256 `7eb1d26af84b5b76134fa73b1121d78d2e043da65a6b3c5cb5f6e6cc2b0aa647`) each contain 1D grid, thermodynamics, composition, mass-radius, initialization, README and provider PDF files. Their datasheets identify the GDFM form but do not list the 12 coefficients or surface fit. This was a scoped inspection, not a proof that no other public or author-held source exists.
- [Carreau et al. 2019, Sec. II.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1902.07032) describes a surface-parameter fit using nine named spherical magic/semi-magic nuclei. It is a related cited method, not evidence that Scurto's 2,408-term Eq. 31 used those nine as its complete sample or that both fit stages use the same cohort. The precise relationship of surface fitting to the 2,408-mass score remains to be established from source implementation or author-provided protocol.
- [Pearson et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.04981) and [Fantina et al.](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/280177/4/doi_263804.pdf) describe 2,408 measured AME2016 masses with N,Z >= 8. That identifies a well-motivated **candidate**, not proof of Scurto's exact row set. AME2016's own header says `#` replaces a decimal point in estimated values. On the pinned file, the rule `N>=8, Z>=8, no # in mass-excess field` yields 2,408 candidate rows and 928 excluded estimated rows. The complete candidate N,Z,A and 1-based line numbers are in `data/receipts/core_crust_source_intake_20261116/AME2016_CANDIDATE.json`, explicitly marked `CANDIDATE_NOT_AUTHOR_AUTHENTICATED`.
