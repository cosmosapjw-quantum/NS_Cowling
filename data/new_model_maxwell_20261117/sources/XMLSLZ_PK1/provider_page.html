XMLSLZ(PK1)

Abstract

This table corresponds to the unified EoS of neutron star (npeµ) matter at zero temperature and β-equilibrium [XMLSL_2022], which is obtained in the framework of Thomas-Fermi approximation and assuming geometrical symmetries for the Wigner-Seitz cells [XSMLL_2022]. The covariant density functional PK1 is adopted [LMGZ_2004].The current version is an update from [NSXM_2025].
