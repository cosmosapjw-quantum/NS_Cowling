APR(APR)

Abstract

This table represents the zero temperature and beta--equilibrium EoS by Akmal, Pandharipande and Ravenhall using variational techniques [APRP_1998], interaction A18 + delta v + UIX*. The inner crust is calculated with SLy4 [DHA_2001], the outer crust from Baym, Pethick, Sutherland [BPSA_1971]. No compositional information is available.
