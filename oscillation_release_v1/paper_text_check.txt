 Quadrupolar f - and p1 -mode frequencies of joined neutron-star equations of state in
                        the relativistic Cowling approximation
                                            [Author name(s) to be supplied]
                                                 (Dated: November 6, 2026)
                We calculate the quadrupolar polar-fluid fundamental mode and first pressure overtone of 234
             crust–core joined neutron-star equations of state (EOSs). The cohort is drawn from a 390-member
             matching matrix containing 13 crust tables, three high-density core families, and six matching pre-
             scriptions; the 156 cases independently classified as retaining source-core acausality are excluded.
             Before stellar integration, the tabulated cold barotropes are represented by a thermodynamically
             consistent, causal piecewise-cubic P (µB ) realization constructed from a monotone q(µB ) = nB /µB .
             The original tables are retained unchanged alongside these derived tables. For each EOS we solve
                                                                                        N
             the TOV equations at a fixed gravitational mass M = 1.3300106263 M⊙           and locate the ℓ = 2
             fluid roots by two-sided matching in the relativistic Cowling approximation. Eigenfunction node
             counts, rather than frequency ordering alone, identify the f and p1 modes. All 234 cases pass
             the node-identity and matching checks. Across the three core families, the median frequencies
             are respectively (f, p1 ) = (2.505, 7.008) kHz for BBB BHF2, (2.370, 6.718) kHz for RG SLY4, and
             (1.920, 6.009) kHz for GM GM1. Tightening the ODE tolerances and step cap changes the frequen-
             cies of three representative models by at most 1.71 × 10−6 Hz between the loose and tight runs.
             These small integration differences quantify numerical convergence within the adopted model; they
             do not quantify the systematic error of the Cowling approximation or the uncertainty associated
             with the barotropic, beta-equilibrated perturbation closure.
