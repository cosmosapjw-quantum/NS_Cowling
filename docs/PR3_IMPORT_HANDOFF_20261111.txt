   (i) reconstruct_master: single-phase F(log n_B) reconstruction.
   (ii) match_crust_core(method="maxwell"): historical smooth near-pressure
        plateau bridge; zero density jump; is_exact_maxwell=false.
   (iii) construct_maxwell_transition: true two-phase, one conserved baryon
         charge, local neutrality explicitly assumed for both phases; equal P
         and Euler mu_B, nonzero density jump and latent-energy identity.
B. Use Euler mu_E=(epsilon+P)/n_B as the true Maxwell independent coordinate.
C. CompOSE Q3 for supported cold branches:
   Il=0: mn*(1+Q3)=mu_E-Yq*mn*Q4.
   Il=1,Q5~0: mn*(1+Q3)=mu_E.
   For Il=0 restore Yq*mn*Q4 before using Q3 to reconstruct mu_E.
   Preserve the convention when writing output Q3.
D. Keep source/reconstruction Q3 tolerance 2e-6 distinct from the true-Maxwell
   v1 kernel local Q3 tolerance 5e-4.
E. Slow/material interface: [xi^r]=0 and [Delta P]=0.
   Rapid interface: [Delta P]=0 and [xi^r-Delta P/P'_0]=0 under the
   exact convention in MAXWELL_TOV_COWLING_INTERFACE_V1.md.
F. Keep one-sided inner/outer values. Never average or interpolate across a
   sharp density jump. Never put finite-difference stencils across Iphase jumps.
G. Slow and rapid spectra are separate datasets. Do not pair modes by frequency
   order outside the explicitly documented small-jump smooth-limit cross-match.
H. Seidov is a GR onset-stability diagnostic with assumptions, not a universal
   stable-branch verdict. No full stable branch/maximum mass claim is inherited.
