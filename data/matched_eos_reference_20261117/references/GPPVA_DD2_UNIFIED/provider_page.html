GPPVA(DD2) NS unified inner crust-core

Abstract

This inner crust-core unified EoS for beta-equilibrium matter at zero temperature is constructed with the BsK22 outer crust EoS by Pearson et al [PCP_2018] below baryon density of 0.002 fm^{−3}, an inner crust EoS, including nonspherical pasta phases, calculated within a self-consistent Thomas-Fermi approach [GPPVP_2014], and a homogeneous npe\mu core EoS, above baryon density of 0.067 fm^{−3}. The EoS parameterisation used was the density-dependent RMF DD2 model [TRKBP_2010].
